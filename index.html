About
We test drinking water. We are licenced to service type II (non-comunity) water supplies. Results are submmitted to Washtenaw County Health Department. Most common analysis is done by Washtenaw County Drinking Water Labratory but not all tests are available a the county level. For less common test samples are mailed to Michigan EAGLE labratory in Lansing, MI.

Pricing is all inclusive. Discounts may apply when scheduling multiple tests. Turn around times may vary but usually take one week.

To schedule a test, call or text: (734) 474-5766

or email: washtenawwatertesting@yahoo.com
